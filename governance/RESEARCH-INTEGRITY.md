# HEXYN LAB — Research Integrity Policy

**Applies to:** KSEL research publications and associated public research artefacts  
**Status:** Public policy, version 1.0  
**Effective date:** 22 November 2026

## 1. Purpose

HEXYN LAB aims to publish work that can be inspected, challenged, corrected and, where feasible, reproduced. This policy defines minimum standards for research claims and publication conduct.

## 2. Claim discipline

Every publication must distinguish:

- **Source evidence:** material, observations, measurements or records used.
- **Reported fact:** a statement directly supported by an identified source or measurement.
- **Interpretation:** an explanation or synthesis of evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** a proposition that remains to be tested.
- **Proposal:** a suggested architecture, method, model or policy.
- **Result:** an outcome produced by a documented method.
- **Recommendation:** a proposed course of action based on stated evidence and assumptions.

A proposed design must not be described as a demonstrated capability. A planned experiment must not be described as a result. A successful implementation test must not be generalised into proof of effectiveness.

## 3. Methods and evidence

Where applicable, publications should disclose:

- Research question and scope.
- Relevant prior work and source references.
- Assumptions and operational definitions.
- Study design, test conditions and exclusions.
- Baselines and comparison methods.
- Dataset provenance, selection and limitations.
- Metrics, calculation methods and uncertainty.
- Software, model and configuration versions.
- Deviations from the planned method.
- Conflicts of interest and material funding or support.

If an item cannot be disclosed for privacy, security, contractual or legal reasons, state the restriction and explain its effect on reproducibility.

## 4. Results and uncertainty

Researchers should report positive, negative, null and inconclusive outcomes. Do not omit unfavourable results solely because they weaken a preferred hypothesis.

Do not invent sample sizes, measurements, citations, benchmark scores, peer reviews or replication claims. Numerical precision must be justified by the method and data. Distinguish statistical uncertainty from measurement, source, model and contextual uncertainty.

## 5. Sources and attribution

Use traceable references for material claims. Attribute ideas, quotations, datasets, software and methods to their creators. Follow applicable licences and permissions. Do not present another party's work as original HEXYN LAB research.

## 6. Corrections and concerns

Material errors should be corrected promptly. Preserve the original publication history and provide a correction note describing what changed, why, and whether conclusions are affected.

Good-faith concerns about methods, attribution, data integrity or conflicts should be reviewed fairly. A concern is not itself proof of misconduct. Where a claim cannot be resolved, document the unresolved status.

## 7. Authorship and contribution

Authorship should reflect substantial intellectual or research contributions and approval of the published work. Other contributions should be acknowledged with permission. Do not add honorary authors or omit contributors who meet the stated criteria.

## 8. Conflicts of interest

Disclose financial, professional, personal or institutional interests that could reasonably be perceived to influence the work. A conflict does not automatically invalidate research; transparency allows readers to assess it.

## 9. Responsible research

Research must respect privacy, safety, applicable law, human-subject protections, data rights and third-party intellectual property. Do not publish personal or confidential data without a lawful and ethical basis. Security-sensitive details should be reviewed before disclosure.

## 10. Policy status

This policy establishes HEXYN LAB's intended publication standard. It does not claim external accreditation, formal ethics-board approval, or certification. It may be revised as the research programme develops.
