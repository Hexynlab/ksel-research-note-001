# HEXYN LAB — Publication and Review Policy

**Status:** Public policy, version 1.0  
**Effective date:** 22 November 2026

## 1. Publication status

Each research artefact should display its title, authorship or issuing institution, version, date, status and scope. Use clear status labels:

- **Draft:** under active preparation; not a final research publication.
- **Preliminary:** published for scrutiny; methods or claims may be incomplete.
- **Protocol:** defines planned methods; does not report completed results.
- **Evaluated:** reports completed work with accessible methods and results.
- **Corrected:** includes a documented correction to a prior version.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a later version; retained for historical reference.

## 2. Versioning

Use explicit version numbers. As a general convention:

- Major version: material change in scope, architecture or conclusions.
- Minor version: substantive additions that preserve the main scope.
- Patch version: corrections, clarifications or editorial fixes that do not materially change conclusions.

Every substantive revision should include a change log. Do not silently overwrite a published research conclusion.

## 3. Review types

Review status must be described accurately:

- **Internal review:** review conducted within HEXYN LAB.
- **External review:** feedback from an identified reviewer outside the laboratory.
- **Independent replication:** a separate party has attempted to reproduce a defined method or result.
- **Formal peer review:** only use this term when a documented peer-review process has actually occurred.

Publication on GitHub or receipt of comments does not constitute peer review.

## 4. Review records

Where permission is granted, publish review summaries that identify the reviewed version, review scope, date, key concerns, author response and disposition. Protect reviewer privacy where requested and do not imply endorsement from participation.

## 5. Corrections, withdrawals and disputes

Corrections should be linked to the affected version. If a publication is withdrawn, retain a notice explaining the reason where legally and ethically appropriate. Disputed claims should be labelled as disputed until resolved; disagreement must not be concealed by editing history.

## 6. Release checklist

Before publication, check:

- Scope and research question are clear.
- Claims are proportionate to the evidence.
- Hypotheses are labelled as hypotheses.
- Methods and limitations are documented.
- Sources and third-party materials are attributed.
- Privacy, security and disclosure risks are reviewed.
- Version, date and status are present.
- Relevant companion notes and citation metadata are linked.

## 7. No implied endorsement

A reviewer, contributor, institution or cited author should not be represented as endorsing HEXYN LAB or KSEL unless explicit permission and evidence of endorsement exist.
