# RN-008 Experiment 001 — Pilot Annotation Packet

**Study ID:** RN-008-EXP-001  
**Packet ID:** RN-008-EXP-001-PILOT-01  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Training/calibration material only — not confirmatory data  
**Institution:** HEXYN LAB

## Purpose and handling

This packet is a small, fully fictional calibration exercise for testing whether annotators can apply the current draft Annotation Handbook consistently. It is not part of the held-out dataset, does not estimate real-world prevalence, and must not be used to tune or evaluate B0, B1, or K1.

Each case contains one claim and one evidence pair. Annotators must work independently and must not consult the separate facilitator answer key until all records are submitted and locked. Use only the records in this packet. Do not browse externally or infer facts not stated here.

For each pair, assign exactly one label:
- DEPENDENT
- INDEPENDENT
- AMBIGUOUS / INSUFFICIENT
- CONFLICTING LINEAGE

Cite the packet record IDs supporting the label. Explain the relationship in relation to the stated claim. If the evidence does not establish a label, preserve uncertainty.

## Case P01 — Direct republication

**Claim P01-C1:** The fictional turbine test reached a peak output of 84 units.

**Evidence P01-E1 — Test bulletin**
- Record ID: P01-R1
- Issuer: North Quay Test Office
- Text: “The turbine test reached a peak output of 84 units.”
- Source history: Original signed test sheet P01-ORIG-01 is identified in the bulletin's attached records register.
- Observation: The office performed and signed the test.

**Evidence P01-E2 — Industry digest**
- Record ID: P01-R2
- Publisher: Field Systems Digest
- Text: “The turbine test reached a peak output of 84 units.”
- Source history: The digest's editorial note states: “This item is reproduced from North Quay Test Office bulletin P01-E1. No separate test was conducted.”
- Observation: No independent measurement is claimed.

**Annotator task:** Classify the relationship between P01-E1 and P01-E2 for claim P01-C1.

**Evidence P02-E3 — Executive brief**

**Pair to annotate:** P02-E1 and P02-E3 for claim P02-C1.  
**Note:** E2 is supplied to show the intermediate lineage step; it is not part of the pair to be labeled.

## Case P03 — Distinct independent observations

- Record ID: P03-R1

- Text: Sensor A recorded 20 degrees at 10:00.
- Source history: Raw data file A-RAW-03 was generated by sensor A, calibrated under certificate CAL-A-03.

**Evidence P03-E2 — Sensor B record**
- Record ID: P03-R2
- Issuer: Instrument Team B
- Text: Sensor B recorded 20 degrees at 10:00.
- Source history: Raw data file B-RAW-03 was generated by sensor B, calibrated under separate certificate CAL-B-03.
- Observation: Sensor B was physically installed at station east.
- Independence record: The deployment log states that the sensors recorded separately, had separate calibration chains, and neither team had access to the other's readings before both files were sealed.

**Annotator task:** Classify the relationship between P03-E1 and P03-E2 for claim P03-C1.

## Case P04 — Shared dataset, unknown use

**Claim P04-C1:** Two fictional analyses independently support a measured value of 5.2 units.

**Evidence P04-E1 — Analysis Alpha**
- Record ID: P04-R1
- Issuer: Delta Research Desk
- Text: “Our analysis reports 5.2 units.”
- Source history: The report cites dataset D-44. It does not disclose whether the team independently reprocessed raw observations, copied a published result, or used a derived extract.

**Evidence P04-E2 — Analysis Beta**
- Record ID: P04-R2
- Issuer: Epsilon Research Desk
- Text: “Our analysis reports 5.2 units.”
- Source history: The report also cites dataset D-44. The access logs, transformation history, and analysis workflow are unavailable. The report does not state whether its result was independently reproduced.

**Packet limitation**
- Dataset D-44 is not included.
- No evidence establishes whether either report used the other's output.
- No separate raw-data processing records are supplied.

**Annotator task:** Classify the relationship between P04-E1 and P04-E2 for claim P04-C1. Do not assume that citing the same dataset necessarily means either dependence or independence.

## Case P06 — Contradictory lineage records

**Claim P06-C1:** The fictional safety result has two independent confirmations.

**Evidence P06-E1 — Archive record**
- Record ID: P06-R1
- Issuer: Archive Unit A
- Text: “The safety test passed.”
- Source history assertion: Signed lineage declaration LA-6 states that P06-E2 was produced by copying P06-E1.

**Evidence P06-E2 — Laboratory record**
- Record ID: P06-R2
- Issuer: Laboratory Unit B
- Text: “The safety test passed.”
- Source history assertion: Signed lineage declaration LB-6 states that P06-E1 was produced by copying P06-E2.

**Packet conflict**
- Both declarations are signed and dated.
- Neither declaration includes the underlying transfer logs.
- The packet contains no independent record that resolves which direction, if either, is correct.
- Both records may share a lineage, but the direction and actual production history are contested.

**Annotator task:** Classify the relationship between P06-E1 and P06-E2 for claim P06-C1. Record the incompatible assertions explicitly.
