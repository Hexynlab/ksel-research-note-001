# Contributing to HEXYN LAB KSEL Research

Thank you for your interest in examining or contributing to the KSEL research programme.

KSEL is a preliminary research programme. Critical review, replication attempts, alternative designs and negative findings are welcome.

## Ways to contribute

- Identify an unclear definition, unsupported claim or missing limitation.
- Suggest relevant literature or competing approaches.
- Challenge a hypothesis and propose a falsifiable alternative.
- Review an evaluation protocol or baseline.
- Reproduce a documented experiment.
- Report a correction with supporting evidence.
- Propose a carefully scoped improvement to a research note.

## Before submitting

Please identify the document and version being discussed. Explain the issue, provide supporting references or evidence, and distinguish factual corrections from differences of interpretation or preference.

For proposed experiments, include the research question, comparison condition, metrics, expected failure modes and relevant ethical or data constraints.

## Review principles

- Be specific, evidence-based and respectful.
- Criticism of an idea is not criticism of a person.
- Do not submit confidential, personal or unlawfully obtained information.
- Do not include credentials, secrets or exploit-enabling details.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Do not claim endorsement or authorship without agreement.
- Verify bibliographic details and cite original or authoritative sources where possible.
- Do not present a citation as proof of a claim unless the cited work actually supports it.

## How to submit

Use GitHub Issues for questions, proposed corrections and research discussion. Use pull requests for concrete text or artefact changes. Keep changes focused and explain their rationale.

Submissions may be discussed, revised, deferred or declined. A contribution does not guarantee publication or authorship. Material accepted into the research record will be attributed appropriately.

## Release contributions

Changes intended for a release must follow the [KSEL Release Governance Policy](governance/RELEASE-GOVERNANCE.md). Update the root `CHANGELOG.md` for material repository changes, preserve individual research-note version histories, and use the [release checklist](governance/RELEASE-CHECKLIST.md) before proposing a release. Do not move or reuse a published release tag.

By submitting material for inclusion, you confirm that you have the necessary rights and agree that accepted material may be distributed under the applicable repository licence. You retain ownership of your contribution. If your contribution contains third-party material or requires different terms, disclose this clearly and obtain approval before submission. Do not assume that submitting a pull request transfers copyright ownership to HEXYN LAB.

For AI-assisted contributions, the submitter must review the output, check the applicable tool terms, and ensure that the contribution does not knowingly include third-party material or restrictions incompatible with the proposed licence. This guide does not determine copyright ownership or replace legal advice.

## Scope

This repository is for KSEL research. Other HEXYN LAB programmes should use their own repositories and governance unless a contribution directly concerns KSEL.
