# KSEL Research Note 001

## Evidence Governance, Uncertainty Management, and Review Controls for Intelligent Systems

**H3XYN LAB — Technical Research Notes**  
**Document ID:** KSEL-RN-001 · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Status:** Preliminary research framework  
**Published:** 22 November 2026

KSEL (Knowledge, Source, Evidence Layer) is a proposed governance architecture for making evidence traceable, uncertainty inspectable, and review decisions auditable in intelligent systems.

This repository contains the first public technical research note defining the framework's scope, design principles, evidence model, uncertainty register, review controls, cost-aware Value of Information (VOI) procedure, audit requirements, and proposed empirical evaluation programme.

**[KSEL Research Note 001 — Full technical paper](research/KSEL-RESEARCH-NOTE-001.md)**

## Research position

This publication is a preliminary architectural proposal. Its hypotheses and evaluation measures are proposed for future testing; they are not presented as validated findings, a certified standard, or proof of system performance.

The note distinguishes source evidence, claims, inference, and recommendations; proposes explicit treatment of uncertainty and contradiction; and defines review states that preserve the limitations and scope of any approval.
